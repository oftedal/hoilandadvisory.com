About

A pragmatic partner for security that earns trust.

Hoiland Advisory is the practice of Elisabeth Høiland, a business-oriented CISO with deep experience leading enterprise security, governance and risk transformation across global technology and industrial environments.

The focus is enabling secure digital transformation by aligning cybersecurity strategy with business priorities, operational resilience and executive risk management, working closely with leadership teams and boards to strengthen organisational trust, resilience and security maturity while supporting innovation and growth.

Known for building collaborative security cultures and pragmatic strategies that balance business enablement with effective risk management. A speaker, advisor and advocate for modern, business-aligned cybersecurity leadership.
